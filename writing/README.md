# Writing workspace

Keep `idea-log.md` chronological and append-only. Refer to proof records by permanent CI ID. Draft text and experimental interpretations go in `drafts/`; a deliberate article structure goes in `outlines/`. Neither is proof evidence. A correction to an earlier observation should identify its date and CI ID and record the revision as a new entry.
